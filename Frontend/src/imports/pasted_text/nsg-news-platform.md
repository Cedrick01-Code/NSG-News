# NSG News — Complete News Platform Development Prompt

Build a **production-ready, modern, full-stack news platform called “NSG News”**. The platform must feel like a premium global digital newsroom while being especially suitable for **Rwanda and African news**. It must be responsive, fast, accessible, SEO-optimized, secure, scalable, and designed with a polished modern UI.

The visual identity should use the **NSG News logo**, a professional **navy + red + white** color system, modern typography, subtle glassmorphism where appropriate, smooth animations, clean cards, strong hierarchy, and a premium editorial aesthetic.
